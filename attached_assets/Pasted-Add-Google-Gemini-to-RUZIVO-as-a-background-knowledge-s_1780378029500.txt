Add Google Gemini to RUZIVO as a background knowledge service.

IMPORTANT RULES:

- DO NOT remove, replace, or modify any existing plant identification functionality.
- DO NOT change any existing UI layouts unless required for the new feature.
- DO NOT display the words:
  - AI
  - Artificial Intelligence
  - Powered by AI
  - Powered by Gemini
  - Gemini
    anywhere in the user interface.

The experience must feel like a natural plant knowledge system.

---

GOAL

Enhance plant information pages with richer educational content and traditional knowledge summaries.

---

STEP 1: ADD GEMINI SERVICE

Create a new service:

src/services/plantKnowledgeService.js

This service should:

- Accept plant identification results
- Generate educational summaries
- Generate traditional-use summaries
- Generate safety notes
- Generate ecological information
- Generate conservation information when available

The service must be separate from existing identification logic.

---

STEP 2: KEEP EXISTING IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM

Do not change:

- Camera scanning
- Plant recognition
- Classification
- Search
- Existing plant database

The current identification system must continue working exactly as it does now.

Gemini should only enrich information after identification is complete.

---

STEP 3: ENHANCE PLANT DETAILS SCREEN

When a plant has been identified, add new sections:

Plant Overview

Traditional Knowledge

Medicinal and Cultural Uses

Safety Information

Habitat and Ecology

Conservation Notes

These sections should appear naturally as part of the plant profile.

Do not mention AI or Gemini.

---

STEP 4: AFRICAN KNOWLEDGE PRIORITY

When generating information:

Prioritize:

- Southern African plant knowledge
- Indigenous knowledge systems
- Traditional uses
- Cultural significance
- Environmental context

Avoid unsupported medical claims.

Use language such as:

"Traditionally used for..."
"Historically associated with..."
"Often used by communities for..."
"Reported traditional uses include..."

Never state that a plant cures or treats diseases.

---

STEP 5: OFFLINE COMPATIBILITY

When internet is available:

Generate enhanced knowledge content.

When internet is unavailable:

Use the existing plant database and offline content.

The application must never fail because the enhancement service is unavailable.

Offline functionality must remain fully operational.

---

STEP 6: CACHING

Store generated plant profiles locally.

If a user previously viewed a plant profile:

Load the cached profile when offline.

This should improve bush/offline usability.

---

STEP 7: USER EXPERIENCE

The user should simply experience:

- Richer plant information
- Better educational content
- More African traditional knowledge
- Improved plant profiles

No technical terms should be shown to users.

---

FINAL REQUIREMENTS

- Do not remove existing features.
- Do not rename existing features.
- Do not add AI branding.
- Do not add Gemini branding.
- Do not change the current plant identification workflow.
- Add functionality only.
- Preserve all existing behaviour.
- Ensure graceful offline fallback.
